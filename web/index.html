Outbreak Nowcaster

Estimate the number of events occurring on recent days after accounting for incomplete reporting. Calculations run locally in your browser.

Illustrative model, not a validated forecast. Observations must be grouped by event date (e.g., symptom onset), not report date. Correction assumes a fixed reporting-delay distribution; defaults are illustrative and should be replaced with locally estimated probabilities. No uncertainty intervals, trend extrapolation, or clinical decision support are provided.

Surveillance input

Use aggregated daily counts of events reported by the snapshot date. CSV columns: date,reported.

Nonnegative weights; normalized to sum to 1. Positive day-zero weight required.
